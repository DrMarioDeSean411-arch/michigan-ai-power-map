Methodology notes
Power-to-water baseline IEA / Congressional Research Service: 100 MW = ~530,000 gal/day. Applied linearly by site capacity.
Gartner growth multiplier Global data center electricity demand: 448 TWh (2025) → 980 TWh (2030). AI servers: 93 TWh → 432 TWh (nearly 5×).
Saline Township dispute Developer claims 20,000 gal/day via closed-loop cooling. IEA methodology for 1,400 MW would project 7.4M gal/day. Developer claim unverified — flagged as disputed.
Flint baseline City of Flint annual municipal water consumption: 3.5 billion gallons (verified utility data). Daily equivalent: 9.59M gal/day.
UN University (June 2026) Global data center water footprint projected at 9.3 trillion liters by 2030 if current trends persist. IEA: 560B liters consumed in 2023, doubling by 2030.
Ypsilanti note Only majority non-white site (42% white, $41K median income). Utility authority imposed 12-month water moratorium April 2026 despite lowest projected consumption in dataset.
